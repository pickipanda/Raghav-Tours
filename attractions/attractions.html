EXPLORE AYODHYA

Top Attractions

Discover the sacred sites, heritage landmarks, and spiritual gems that make Ayodhya one of India's most revered destinations.

Temple

Ram Mandir

A Spiritual Epicenter

The newly built Ram Mandir at Ram Janmabhoomi is one of the most significant religious structures in India. Standing tall with Nagara-style architecture, it draws millions of devotees and visitors every year to experience its divine presence.

🕐 Open: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM 📍 Ram Janmabhoomi, Ayodhya
Temple

Hanuman Garhi

76-step Climb to Devotion

Perched atop a hill, Hanuman Garhi is a fortress-temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman. Climb its 76 iconic steps to reach the sanctum and enjoy panoramic views of Ayodhya. Considered the guardian of the city, the temple is a must-visit.

🕐 Open: 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM 📍 Hanuman Garhi Road, Ayodhya
Temple

Hanuman Mandir

Abode of Devotion

A vibrant hub of spiritual activity where devotees gather daily to offer prayers and seek blessings. The temple is alive with the sound of bhajans and the fragrance of incense, creating an unforgettable spiritual atmosphere.

🕐 Open: 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM 📍 Ayodhya City Centre
Temple

Kanak Bhavan

A Royal Abode

Known for its exquisite golden architecture and divine idols of Lord Rama and Sita, Kanak Bhavan is one of the most beautiful temples in Ayodhya. Its ornate interiors and peaceful ambiance offer a glimpse into royal heritage.

🕐 Open: 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM, 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM 📍 Near Ram Janmabhoomi
Heritage

Kanak Bhavan — Divine Art

Architectural Masterpiece

Step inside Kanak Bhavan to admire breathtaking frescoes, intricate carvings, and golden ornaments adorning every wall. The temple's art narrates the timeless story of Rama and Sita through colour and craftsmanship.

🎨 Architecture: Awadhi Style 📍 Ayodhya
Nature

Sarayu River

The Sacred Waters

The Sarayu is the life-giving river of Ayodhya, mentioned in the Ramayana as the sacred river of Lord Rama. Evening aarti on its ghats is a mesmerising experience — golden lamps reflecting on the flowing waters create an otherworldly sight.

🕐 Aarti: 6:30 AM & 6:30 PM 📍 Ram Ki Paidi Ghat
Nature

Spiritual Morning Walk

Dawn at the Ghat

Begin your day with a peaceful morning walk along the Sarayu ghats. Watch pilgrims take holy dips, witness priests perform dawn rituals, and soak in the tranquil sunrise over the sacred waters. A deeply calming and spiritual experience.

🌅 Best Time: 5:30 AM – 7:30 AM 📍 Sarayu Ghats, Ayodhya
Temple

Ram Mandir Darshan

The Grand Pilgrimage

A guided visit to Ram Mandir with our expert team ensures you don't miss any important corners of this magnificent temple. We help you navigate queues, explain architectural details, and make your darshan meaningful and memorable.

🕐 Darshan: 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM 📍 Ram Janmabhoomi Marg
Heritage

Ayodhya Cultural Street

Living Heritage

Stroll through Ayodhya's vibrant cultural streets lined with traditional shops selling handcrafted artefacts, religious souvenirs, street food, and folk art. The streets come alive with music and colour, especially during festivals.

🛍️ Best for: Shopping & Local Food 📍 Near Ram Janmabhoomi
